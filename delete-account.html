Delete Your Account & Data — Madzie

Madzie is built around local-first storage — there is no Madzie-run server or account database. Your journal data lives on your device, and optionally in a private folder in your own iCloud or Google Drive account. Deleting it is entirely self-service, in two parts.

1. Delete your journal data

  1. Open Madzie and go to Settings → Reset and Data Management → Reset Journal Data.
  2. Confirm you understand the consequences, then hold the delete button for 5 seconds.

This permanently deletes all journal entries from your device. If cloud sync is enabled, the same deletion is pushed to your private iCloud or Google Drive storage on the next sync, replacing the stored copy. Nothing is retained by Madzie anywhere else — there is no separate backup or retention period, since Madzie never had a copy to begin with.

2. Revoke Madzie's access to your Google account (Android)

If you enabled Google Drive sync, Madzie authorizes access via "Sign in with Google." To fully disconnect this at the source:

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Find Madzie in the list of apps with account access.
  3. Remove its access.

This immediately revokes Madzie's ability to read or write to your Drive storage. Apple users manage the equivalent iCloud access under their device's Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
